## A1 — The Brief

**Positioning statement (one line):**
> Channakeshava Gowda is an engineering manager who turns ambiguous, high-stakes systems
> problems into calm, well-run teams and platforms that scale — from 3,000 live EV chargers
> to AI that runs verification work on its own.

**Who it's for:** hiring leaders, senior recruiters, founders, and peer engineering
managers. They skim first, read second. They're deciding *"can this person own a hard
problem and a team at once?"* in under 30 seconds.

**What they must feel:** *considered.* The site itself is proof of taste, clarity, and
execution — the same qualities CG brings to a team. It should feel like a well-designed
instrument: nothing extra, everything intentional.

**Tone words (the north star for every decision):**
`precise` · `warm` · `engineered` · `calm` · `confident`

> Precise but not cold. Warm but not casual. Engineered but not busy. The robot carries the
> warmth so the layout can stay disciplined.

**"We are / we are not":**
- We are **an instrument**, not a poster.
- We are **quietly confident**, not loud.
- We are **an operator who ships**, not a buzzword résumé.

## A3 — Brand Voice

**How the copy sounds:** plain, exact, quietly assured. Short sentences. Concrete nouns and
numbers over adjectives. Never salesy. An engineer wrote this and respects your time.

**Label style:** lowercase-technical for eyebrows, nav, and metadata — like markings on
hardware (`about`, `experience`, `impact`, `contact`). Headlines can be sentence case.

**Voice do / don't:**
- ✅ "led a 12-person team through an EKS migration serving 5M+ requests a day."
- ❌ "passionate, results-driven leader leveraging synergies to drive impact."
- ✅ numbers stated plainly: `3,000+ concurrent connections`.
- ❌ exclamation marks, hype, or filler.

**The robot's voice (mascot):** friendly, minimal, a little dry. It's the warmth budget of
an otherwise disciplined site — appears at the hero and the sign-off, never explains itself.
