A (Inter + JetBrains Mono) — Inter is the most neutral, best-hinted UI grotesque available; it disappears and lets the content speak. JetBrains Mono has a slightly wider, warmer character than most monos, which suits the cream base. B (Geist) — one designed family for both roles, so sans and mono share skeletons; slightly cooler and more "product". Both are correct — A is warmer, B is tighter.
